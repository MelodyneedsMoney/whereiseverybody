Visual renderer and assets adapted from ConcatenationGAN / Latent Spacecraft:
https://latentspacecraft.antikythera.org/concatenationgan/

Original page credits: Nina Beguš, Metahaven, Gašper Beguš and Riccardo Petrini.
Subtitle styling and the Chicago Plain font are taken from the same reference.
Font source: https://latentspacecraft.antikythera.org/assets/Chicago-DSaQPV7k.woff
The current DEAD AIR adaptation retains the three rendering layers, film and
afterimage effects, procedural geometry, and two monochrome LUT assets. LUT
files retain their original ACIDBITE copyright headers. Input, idle timing,
screen tearing, and interface have been modified for DEAD AIR.
